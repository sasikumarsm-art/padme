Our Approach – The TEA Model

Thoughts. Emotions. Actions. Understanding how internal mindsets and emotional experiences interlock to drive external leadership choices.

T
Pillar 1

Thoughts

"What Am I Telling Myself?"

Every decision originates in a cognitive narrative. Unexamined assumptions, implicit bias and internal critical dialog often constrain possibilities long before action is taken.

Through structured inquiry, leaders examine their mental models, replace self-limiting narratives with objective reality, and make choices grounded in clarity rather than fear.

Key Inquiries in Thoughts Coaching

  • Identifying subconscious core assumptions guiding strategic choices.
  • Separating objective facts from internal interpretations under pressure.
  • Reframing perfectionism and imposter loops into constructive iteration.

Key Inquiries in Emotional Intelligence

  • Developing emotional granular awareness and somatic self-regulation.
  • Recognizing stress triggers before they lead to knee-jerk reactions.
  • Creating psychological safety for team members during organizational shifts.
E
Pillar 2

Emotions

"What Am I Experiencing?"

Emotions are data — vital signals about value alignment, risk, boundary breaches or exhaustion. Suppressing emotion leads to burnout, while unmanaged emotion leads to volatile leadership.

We guide leaders to listen to emotional signals with curiosity, developing emotional literacy and composure under high pressure.

A
Pillar 3

Actions

"How Am I Choosing to Respond?"

Action is the external embodiment of inner clarity. When thoughts are lucid and emotions are understood, actions become intentional, courageous and consistent.

We partner with executives to translate heightened awareness into sustainable daily habits, clear boundaries and decisive leadership execution.

Key Outcomes in Aligned Action

  • Communicating with authentic authority and empathetic presence.
  • Setting firm professional boundaries to protect energy and strategic focus.
  • Sustaining long-term organizational transformation without burnout.
