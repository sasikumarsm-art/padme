Founder & Facilitator

Anushree Jain Lingesh

Bridging Technology Leadership & Personal Transformation

Anushree Jain Lingesh is the founder of Padme Oasis, an executive leadership coach and personal transformation practitioner. She works with senior professionals, technology leaders and executive teams seeking clarity, resilience and conscious leadership.

Verified Professional Background

  • Technology Leadership: Prior engineering and technical experience at global technology leaders IBM and Arm in embedded technologies.
  • Coaching Credentials: Over 1,000+ hours of direct coaching experience; completed ICF ACC and PCC coaching qualification programs.
  • Holistic Practice: 12 years of practice in Pranic Healing and Arhatic Yoga, integrating energetic awareness with pragmatic executive coaching.
  • Industry Recognition: Recipient of the HerKey Award 2025 for women and impact in mental and emotional wellness.

"My mission is to create a safe, clear space where leaders can step out of relentless execution, look inward, and align their actions with their deepest truth."
